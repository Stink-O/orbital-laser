## New

- **Planet Killer**, on the wheel's second page and `/orbital planetkiller`:
  - seven towers over 400 blocks tall break out of the land about a kilometre off, one after another, and each strikes the ring over the target with a bolt of lightning;
  - the ring fuses and fires one beam down, and the land under it domes up and bursts;
  - it leaves a crater about 480 blocks across with a shaft to bedrock, a broken rim of thrown rock, twelve chasms and charred land round it;
  - then the ring, crushed to a point, falls into the crater and bursts again;
  - its sounds are its own, and its explosion is modelled on measurements of a real one.
- **Faster terrain.** The Planet Killer writes its crater straight into the world's chunks through the fork's new `world.edit-blocks` instead of thousands of commands, so there is far less stutter. Its land is done about 25 seconds into the strike.
- **The wheel fits any window**, down to the smallest GUI (320 × 240), with short blurbs and tidier spacing.

## Install

1. **Get the Orbital Laser build of Horizon, `orbital-mod-api-4`** (it adds `world.edit-blocks`; older builds won't start the mod): download `horizon-orbital-linux-x86_64.tar.gz` from [Stink-O/horizon releases](https://github.com/Stink-O/horizon/releases) and unpack it to `~/horizon-orbital`. Prepare its resources once from your own Minecraft 26.3 (`./horizon-prepare --prepare --server-jar PATH/TO/server.jar`), or point `HORIZON_RESOURCES` at the prepared resources of a Horizon you already have.
2. Download **orbital-laser-v0.5.2.zip** below, unzip it, and with the game closed run `python3 install.py`.
3. Run `./play.sh` (a creative test world) or `./play.sh MyWorld`.
4. In the game, type `/orbital give`. Press **R** (or right-click the Orbital Remote) for the laser wheel, and hold right-click with the Designator on a target to fire. The Planet Killer is on the wheel's second page.

**Want an AI agent to do it?** Paste the prompt from the [README](https://github.com/Stink-O/orbital-laser#set-it-up-with-an-ai-agent) into your coding agent.

> [!WARNING]
> **The Planet Killer is the most destructive laser yet, and it is permanent.** It flattens everything above the ground out to 340 blocks, digs a crater 480 across and 80 deep with a shaft to bedrock, heaps a rim of rock out to 340, splits the land with chasms out to about 400, chars it out to 380 and kills every mob within 280. Stand at least 450 blocks back, back up your world first, or switch terrain damage off on the wheel.

> [!WARNING]
> **Early and experimental.** Developed and tested on one Linux PC (Fedora, NVIDIA). Windows and macOS are untested, and the ready-built Horizon is Linux x86-64 only.
