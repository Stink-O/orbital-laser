## New

- **Crystal Bloom**, on the wheel's second page and `/orbital crystalbloom`:
  - crystals heave up out of cracked ground with dust and earth, then 46 refractive spires bloom out over the land;
  - they resonate with prism beams and rainbow light on the land, under an aurora;
  - a few glowing cracks open in them, then they shatter into about 200 fractured pieces, and each one lands as blocks of its own shape, resting on the ground where it falls;
  - it leaves a glowing geode crater with a crystal-lined well to bedrock, broken crystal pillars and shards all round;
  - the strike lasts 60 seconds, and its sounds are new, synthesised from struck glass.
- **Strikes tidy up after themselves.** Loose plants, leaves and the trees a blast cuts through are cleared at its edge, so nothing is left hanging. The Planet Killer does this too.
- **Faster.** The Crystal Bloom's pass reads a table the mod works out once a strike, through the fork's new `render.set-data`, which roughly halves its GPU time at the heaviest moments.

## Install

1. **Get the Orbital Laser build of Horizon, `orbital-mod-api-5`** (it adds `world.surface-height` and `render.set-data`; `orbital-mod-api-4` and older won't start the mod): download `horizon-orbital-linux-x86_64.tar.gz` from [Stink-O/horizon releases](https://github.com/Stink-O/horizon/releases) and unpack it to `~/horizon-orbital`. Prepare its resources once from your own Minecraft 26.3 (`./horizon-prepare --prepare --server-jar PATH/TO/server.jar`), or point `HORIZON_RESOURCES` at the prepared resources of a Horizon you already have.
2. Download **orbital-laser-v0.5.3.zip** below, unzip it, and with the game closed run `python3 install.py`.
3. Run `./play.sh` (a creative test world) or `./play.sh MyWorld`.
4. In the game, type `/orbital give`. Press **R** (or right-click the Orbital Remote) for the laser wheel, and hold right-click with the Designator on a target to fire. The Crystal Bloom is on the wheel's second page.

**Want an AI agent to do it?** Paste the prompt from the [README](https://github.com/Stink-O/orbital-laser#set-it-up-with-an-ai-agent) into your coding agent.

> [!WARNING]
> **The Crystal Bloom is permanent.** It clears everything above the ground out to about 130 blocks, digs a geode crater about 176 across and 28 deep round a well to bedrock, leaves crystal pillars out to 150, opens fissures out to about 205, strews shards out to 235 and kills every mob within 160. Stand at least 250 blocks back, back up your world first, or switch terrain damage off on the wheel.

> [!WARNING]
> **Early and experimental.** Developed and tested on one Linux PC (Fedora, NVIDIA). Windows and macOS are untested, and the ready-built Horizon is Linux x86-64 only.
