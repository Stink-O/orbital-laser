A third laser: **Black Hole**. A singularity snaps open over the target, bends the light round it, rips the terrain up into orbit and swallows it.

## Install

1. **Get the Orbital Laser build of Horizon:** download `horizon-orbital-linux-x86_64.tar.gz` from [Stink-O/horizon releases](https://github.com/Stink-O/horizon/releases) and unpack it to `~/horizon-orbital`. Prepare its resources once from your own Minecraft 26.3 (`./horizon-prepare --prepare --server-jar PATH/TO/server.jar`), or point `HORIZON_RESOURCES` at the prepared resources of a Horizon you already have.
2. Download **orbital-laser-v0.3.0.zip** below, unzip it, and with the game closed run `python3 install.py`.
3. Run `./play.sh` (a creative test world) or `./play.sh MyWorld`.
4. In the game, type `/orbital give`. Press **R** (or right-click the Orbital Remote) for the laser wheel, and hold right-click with the Designator on a target to fire.

**Stuck, or want an AI agent to do it?** Paste the prompt from the [README](https://github.com/Stink-O/orbital-laser#set-it-up-with-an-ai-agent) into your coding agent.

**Needs:** the Orbital Laser build of Horizon (with your own copy of Minecraft Java 26.3) and Python 3.8 or newer for the installer.

> [!WARNING]
> **A strike is destructive and permanent.** It flattens everything above the ground within about 44 blocks (52 for the Black Hole), digs a crater about 60 blocks across and a shaft down to bedrock, and kills every mob and dropped item within 40 blocks (players are spared). Back up your world first, or switch terrain damage off on the wheel.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Stock Horizon can't run this mod.** Horizon's mod API doesn't yet let a mod catch an item's use, open its own screen, draw effects or change the world. The [Orbital Laser build](https://github.com/Stink-O/horizon/tree/mod-api-input-ui-render-world) is a Horizon fork that adds those four, from Horizon's own roadmap. On stock Horizon, `python3 install.py --legacy` sets up 0.1's way of doing it instead.

> [!WARNING]
> **Early and experimental.** Developed and tested on one Linux PC (Fedora, NVIDIA). Windows and macOS are untested, and the ready-built Horizon is Linux x86-64 only.

## New

- **Black Hole**, the third laser, on the wheel and `/orbital blackhole`:
  - the sky goes dark and starry, a violet spiral winds in on the ground, and the whole picture is sucked in towards one point;
  - then it snaps open 28 blocks up: the picture flashes negative and a ripple of bent space tears across the screen;
  - its light bending is traced for real, so the land and sky behind it warp round it, and its accretion disk shows its far side over the top and under the bottom, brighter on the side turning towards you;
  - a jet pours out of both poles and bores down to bedrock;
  - it evaporates in a blinding flash after about ten seconds.
- **Terrain ripped into orbit.** 160 blocks and chunks are torn out of the ground round the crater (the real ones are removed as they go) and tumble up round the hole. Most stretch, glow red-hot and are swallowed at the horizon, some are slingshotted away, and real blocks spat out of the disk come down as rubble across the land.
- **What it leaves:** the land dragged into spiral arms of sculk and black concrete, streams of rock reaching up towards where it hung, a crater lined with obsidian and crying obsidian, and a ring of rock left hanging where the disk was.
- **Its own sounds**, synthesized from scratch: an inhale that cuts dead, a vast low blast, the hole's drone, the evaporation.
- The Black Hole is mod-only: the 0.1 setup for stock Horizon still has Plasma and Godly.
