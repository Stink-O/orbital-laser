## Install

1. **Get the Orbital Laser build of Horizon:** download `horizon-orbital-linux-x86_64.tar.gz` from [Stink-O/horizon releases](https://github.com/Stink-O/horizon/releases) and unpack it to `~/horizon-orbital`. Prepare its resources once from your own Minecraft 26.3 (`./horizon-prepare --prepare --server-jar PATH/TO/server.jar`), or point `HORIZON_RESOURCES` at the prepared resources of a Horizon you already have.
2. Download **orbital-laser-v0.4.0.zip** below, unzip it, and with the game closed run `python3 install.py`.
3. Run `./play.sh` (a creative test world) or `./play.sh MyWorld`.
4. In the game, type `/orbital give`. Press **R** (or right-click the Orbital Remote) for the laser wheel, and hold right-click with the Designator on a target to fire.

**Stuck, or want an AI agent to do it?** Paste the prompt from the [README](https://github.com/Stink-O/orbital-laser#set-it-up-with-an-ai-agent) into your coding agent.

**Needs:** the Orbital Laser build of Horizon (with your own copy of Minecraft Java 26.3) and Python 3.8 or newer for the installer.

> [!WARNING]
> **A strike is destructive and permanent.** It flattens everything above the ground within about 44 blocks (52 for the Black Hole), digs a crater about 60 blocks across and a shaft down to bedrock, and kills every mob and dropped item within 40 blocks (players are spared). **The Meteor Storm is far bigger:** craters across 120 blocks, everything above the ground flattened out to 100 blocks, a crater about 125 blocks across, the land charred out to 135, rubble thrown out to 250 and every mob within 130 blocks killed. Back up your world first, or switch terrain damage off on the wheel.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Stock Horizon can't run this mod.** Horizon's mod API doesn't yet let a mod catch an item's use, open its own screen, draw effects or change the world. The [Orbital Laser build](https://github.com/Stink-O/horizon/tree/mod-api-input-ui-render-world) is a Horizon fork that adds those four, from Horizon's own roadmap. On stock Horizon, `python3 install.py --legacy` sets up 0.1's way of doing it instead.

> [!WARNING]
> **Early and experimental.** Developed and tested on one Linux PC (Fedora, NVIDIA). Windows and macOS are untested, and the ready-built Horizon is Linux x86-64 only.

## New

- **Meteor Storm**, the fourth laser, on the wheel and `/orbital meteor`:
  - 72 meteors, from tiny to big, come in from high in the atmosphere along curved paths, burning, with trails of fire and smoke that hang in the sky;
  - they land scattered and in clusters across 120 blocks round the target, each with a flash, a ground shockwave and a dust skirt, and each blows out a real round crater with a meteorite at the bottom, a lip of rubble and fires;
  - then the big one: a white-out, and a fireball that tears open in a different shape every time and throws glowing ejecta out every way;
  - everything above the ground goes out to 100 blocks, a crater about 125 blocks across is blown out with a raised rim, a shaft to bedrock and lava on its floor, and the land is charred out to 135;
  - real blocks are thrown out as far as 250 blocks, and ash comes down over everything.
- **Its own sounds**, made to sound like real footage rather than a game: a whoosh as each meteor comes in, hits sized to the meteor that arrive later and muffled the further off they land (sound travels at its real speed), and a deep blast for the big one with rumbling debris after it.
- **Faster strikes.** Block changes are spread over the ticks (up to 20,000 blocks a tick) instead of landing all at once, the biggest stages are worked out only when they're due, the post pass is warmed up when the mod loads so the first strike doesn't hitch, and the Black Hole costs less to draw.
- The Meteor Storm is mod-only: the 0.1 setup for stock Horizon still has Plasma and Godly.
