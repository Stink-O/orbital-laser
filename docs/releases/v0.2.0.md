Orbital Laser is now a mod that runs inside the game. Hold the Orbital Laser Designator on a target, and a beam drops from orbit and digs a crater and a shaft down to bedrock. Pick the laser from a wheel: **Plasma** or the new **Godly**.

## Install

1. **Get the Orbital Laser build of Horizon:** download `horizon-orbital-linux-x86_64.tar.gz` from [Stink-O/horizon releases](https://github.com/Stink-O/horizon/releases) and unpack it to `~/horizon-orbital`. Prepare its resources once from your own Minecraft 26.3 (`./horizon-prepare --prepare --server-jar PATH/TO/server.jar`), or point `HORIZON_RESOURCES` at the prepared resources of a Horizon you already have.
2. Download **orbital-laser-v0.2.0.zip** below, unzip it, and with the game closed run `python3 install.py`.
3. Run `./play.sh` (a creative test world) or `./play.sh MyWorld`.
4. In the game, type `/orbital give`. Press **R** (or right-click the Orbital Remote) for the laser wheel, and hold right-click with the Designator on a target to fire.

**Stuck, or want an AI agent to do it?** Paste the prompt from the [README](https://github.com/Stink-O/orbital-laser#set-it-up-with-an-ai-agent) into your coding agent.

**Needs:** the Orbital Laser build of Horizon (with your own copy of Minecraft Java 26.3) and Python 3.8 or newer for the installer.

> [!WARNING]
> **A strike is destructive and permanent.** It flattens everything above the ground within about 44 blocks, digs a crater about 60 blocks across and a shaft down to bedrock, and kills every mob and dropped item within 40 blocks (players are spared). Back up your world first, or switch terrain damage off on the wheel.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Stock Horizon can't run this mod.** Horizon's mod API doesn't yet let a mod catch an item's use, open its own screen, draw effects or change the world. The [Orbital Laser build](https://github.com/Stink-O/horizon/tree/mod-api-input-ui-render-world) is a Horizon fork that adds those four, from Horizon's own roadmap. On stock Horizon, `python3 install.py --legacy` sets up 0.1's way of doing it instead.

> [!WARNING]
> **Early and experimental.** Developed and tested on one Linux PC (Fedora, NVIDIA). Windows and macOS are untested, and the ready-built Horizon is Linux x86-64 only.

## New

- **A real mod.** The strike, the crater and the sounds run inside the game, with no script and nothing in chat.
- **Real items:** the Orbital Laser Designator and the Orbital Remote, with their own textures, and `/orbital give` to get them.
- **The laser wheel**, on the remote or **R**: Plasma, Godly, and terrain damage on or off in the middle. Rebind R in **Options → Controls → Key Binds**.
- **Godly**, the new laser:
  - a golden sigil burns into the ground while the clouds roll in and tear open overhead;
  - a calm pillar of light comes down, with halo rings and wings standing in the sky;
  - six pillars of light land round it and a dome of light swells out;
  - it leaves a crater of quartz and gold, the sigil etched in gold, six quartz pillars, a wave of flowers and chunks of rock floating above the crater.
- **Plasma**, reworked:
  - a 3D fireball that climbs into a mushroom cloud;
  - the crater's walls fused into obsidian, black glass and magma;
  - lava pools that crust over into obsidian, fires round the rim, and rubble coming down across the land.
- **Everything is in the world.** The effects stay put when you move and sit behind hills in front of them. Nothing is painted flat on the screen but the lens.
- **No packs to select.** The effects are the mod's own post-processing pass and the sounds ship inside the mod.
- **A status panel** while you hold either item: the loaded laser and whether terrain damage is on.
