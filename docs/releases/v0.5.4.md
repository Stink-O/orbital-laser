## New

- **Sinkhole**, on the wheel's second page and `/orbital sinkhole`:
  - a seismic warning, a deep rumble you feel more than hear, and birds going up off the land and scattering;
  - cracks race out over the land in spurts, along the lines it will break on;
  - at 6 seconds the middle gives, then the land caves in from the middle outward in slabs that carry the real terrain (its blocks, biome colours and trees) as they tip and drop into a dark pit;
  - dust boils up out of the hole and rolls out over the land, then an echoing near-silence and pebbles running down the walls;
  - it leaves a sheer pit to bedrock, about 225 blocks across, its walls banded and darkening downward, with ledges, roots under the overhangs and waterfalls where it cut a river or a lake;
  - the strike lasts 40 seconds, and its sounds are new, synthesised from rock and earth.
- **Real land on the slabs.** The mod reads the blocks and biome colours of every column it is about to drop through the fork's new `world.read-columns`, so each falling slab looks like the ground it was cut from.

## Install

1. **Get the Orbital Laser build of Horizon, `orbital-mod-api-6`** (it adds `world.read-columns`; `orbital-mod-api-5` and older won't start the mod): download `horizon-orbital-linux-x86_64.tar.gz` from [Stink-O/horizon releases](https://github.com/Stink-O/horizon/releases) and unpack it to `~/horizon-orbital`. Prepare its resources once from your own Minecraft 26.3 (`./horizon-prepare --prepare --server-jar PATH/TO/server.jar`), or point `HORIZON_RESOURCES` at the prepared resources of a Horizon you already have.
2. Download **orbital-laser-v0.5.4.zip** below, unzip it, and with the game closed run `python3 install.py`.
3. Run `./play.sh` (a creative test world) or `./play.sh MyWorld`.
4. In the game, type `/orbital give`. Press **R** (or right-click the Orbital Remote) for the laser wheel, and hold right-click with the Designator on a target to fire. The Sinkhole is on the wheel's second page.

**Want an AI agent to do it?** Paste the prompt from the [README](https://github.com/Stink-O/orbital-laser#set-it-up-with-an-ai-agent) into your coding agent.

> [!WARNING]
> **The Sinkhole is permanent.** It drops everything over a pit about 225 blocks across (its rim wanders from about 190 to 260) down to bedrock, up to the top of whatever stood there, mountains and trees included, and kills every mob within 140. Stand at least 200 blocks back, back up your world first, or switch terrain damage off on the wheel.

> [!WARNING]
> **Early and experimental.** Developed and tested on one Linux PC (Fedora, NVIDIA). Windows and macOS are untested, and the ready-built Horizon is Linux x86-64 only.
