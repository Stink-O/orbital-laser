## New

- **Payload**, on the wheel's second page and `/orbital payload`:
  - a few seconds of calm, then a small, fiercely bright point shows high in the sky and drifts across it on a lazy path, trailing lumpy smoke that frays and thins away over twenty seconds;
  - it dives as a fireball, burning chunks and sparks breaking off it, and you hear it crackle overhead as it tears through the air;
  - it hits: a flash, then a huge ragged column of tan smoke rises out of the crater, sunlit and lit by the time of day, warm at dusk and faint under the moon at night;
  - a wall of dust hugs the land as it races out, with a camera shake and muck on the lens as it passes, debris and real blocks thrown out of the crater;
  - the trees round it are felled pointing away from the blast, and it leaves a scorched crater about 42 blocks across;
  - the strike lasts 70 seconds. Its sounds are new and synthesised, and they arrive late by the distance.
- **Two blast sounds** for the Payload, MAIN or ALT: the wheel's BLAST SOUND row (while the Payload is loaded or pointed at), or `/orbital payloadsound main|alt`.
- **STOP STRIKE** on the wheel (top left while a strike is under way) and `/orbital stop`: the strike ends there and then, its sounds cut and its waiting world changes dropped. What it has already done to the world stays.
- **QUALITY FAST or FULL** on the wheel and `/orbital quality fast|full`. FAST, the default, draws the Payload's smoke at half size, about 4 ms a frame at 1080p instead of 11. It uses the half-size stage new in the fork's `orbital-mod-api-7`; on `orbital-mod-api-6` FAST draws at full size, the same as FULL.

## Install

1. **Get the Orbital Laser build of Horizon, `orbital-mod-api-7`** (it adds a half-size stage for a mod's post pass; `orbital-mod-api-6` also runs this release, with the Payload's smoke always at full size, and older builds won't start the mod): download `horizon-orbital-linux-x86_64.tar.gz` from [Stink-O/horizon releases](https://github.com/Stink-O/horizon/releases) and unpack it to `~/horizon-orbital`. Prepare its resources once from your own Minecraft 26.3 (`./horizon-prepare --prepare --server-jar PATH/TO/server.jar`), or point `HORIZON_RESOURCES` at the prepared resources of a Horizon you already have.
2. Download **orbital-laser-v0.5.5.zip** below, unzip it, and with the game closed run `python3 install.py`.
3. Run `./play.sh` (a creative test world) or `./play.sh MyWorld`.
4. In the game, type `/orbital give`. Press **R** (or right-click the Orbital Remote) for the laser wheel, and hold right-click with the Designator on a target to fire. The Payload is on the wheel's second page.

**Want an AI agent to do it?** Paste the prompt from the [README](https://github.com/Stink-O/orbital-laser#set-it-up-with-an-ai-agent) into your coding agent.

> [!WARNING]
> **The Payload is permanent.** It digs a crater about 42 blocks across and 9 deep with its rim heaped out to about 35 from the middle, sweeps everything standing on the land off out to about 85 (the trees felled), chars the grass out to 100, strews rubble out to about 105 and kills every mob within 90. Stand at least 150 blocks back, back up your world first, or switch terrain damage off on the wheel.

> [!WARNING]
> **Early and experimental.** Developed and tested on one Linux PC (Fedora, NVIDIA). Windows and macOS are untested, and the ready-built Horizon is Linux x86-64 only.
