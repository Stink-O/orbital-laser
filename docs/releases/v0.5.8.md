A new laser, the **Hurricane**, and the pop-up messages are gone.

The **Hurricane** spins a hurricane up over the target, some 3,500 blocks across. Storms gather and wind into spiral rain bands, the eye opens into a stadium of cloud with clear sky overhead, and lightning bursts in the eyewall. Then the wind tears the land up round the eyewall and whirls it round the eye: trees, buildings, the ground's top blocks, thousands of real blocks in the game's own textures. It picks you up and carries you round with them. At 46 seconds it throws everything out, and it all comes down as real blocks.

> [!CAUTION]
> **Photosensitivity:** the Hurricane's eyewall lightning is bright flashes over much of the picture, in bursts at about 16-28 and 43-46 seconds into the strike. The Rod from God's impact frames are about a fifth of a second of hard full-screen cuts between light and dark, on by default (turn them off with `/orbital impactframes off` or OFF on the wheel's IMPACT FX row). The Supercell's finale is a rapid run of lightning flashes, and other strikes have bright flashes of their own (lightning, blasts).

## New

- **Hurricane**, on the wheel's third page and `/orbital hurricane`:
  - **the storm:** a ragged mass of towering storms gathers over the target and is drawn into three trailing rain bands wound tighter and tighter round a dense core. The eye opens from a pinhole into a stadium: its wall flaring out as it rises, its far side lit by the sun, clear sky straight overhead. From above it is one shield of cloud with the bands feathering out of it and cirrus streaming over the top. Rain falls round you in the world (the world's own weather is left alone), none in the eye;
  - **the lightning:** bursts of it in the eyewall as the storm builds and again before the end, some bolts reaching out of the eyewall's side across the open moat round it, with thunder arriving late with the distance;
  - **the tear-up:** from 15 seconds the wind's front tears at the land from the eyewall out to about 150 blocks: the plants and leaves first, then the trees, everything built (roofs first, then walls) and the ground's top one to three blocks. Nothing pops into the air: every block rises out of the land behind the front and climbs round the eyewall. Trees meet a mix of fates: most are shredded where they stand, some go up whole, some snap at the trunk and leave a stump, and some break apart in the air. The blocks are drawn with the game's own textures, leaves and glass see-through as the game draws them;
  - **the carry:** mobs and dropped items are carried round with it, and so are you (with KNOCKBACK on). The wind picks you up when its front reaches you, wherever you are and whatever you are doing, eases you into the spin and carries you round the eyewall with the debris, about 108 blocks out and 50 up. Your view turns a little with you and sways in the gusts; `/orbital carryview still` keeps it your own;
  - **breaking free:** hold **sneak** for half a second. A creative player is put back into flight. While you fly the wind leaves you alone, but stop flying for a second (land, or drop out of flight) while it still blows where you are and it grabs you again;
  - **the throw:** at 46 seconds the winds drop and everything is flung out. The flying blocks shatter to dust where they hit, the trees and pieces of buildings land as real blocks lying where they fall, rubble rains down out to about 170 blocks, and you are thrown clear at about 47 seconds. A player who can't fly gets 12 seconds of Resistance V so the landing doesn't kill them. Then the eyewall collapses and the cloud thins away;
  - **the sound:** continuous wind, the eyewall's roar and the rain through the storm's whole life, debris and trees cracking now and then, whooshes going past while you're carried, and thunder for the lightning;
  - **the land:** no crater, but everything standing round the eyewall goes, out to about 150 blocks for trees and buildings and 175 for plants. The calm eye (26 blocks across the middle) is untouched. Water, unbreakable blocks, blocks from other mods, and chests and the like (with what's in them) stay where they are. Mobs aren't killed outright: they're thrown, and the fall hurts. It works on far-off targets, as the Nuke does;
  - the strike lasts 64 seconds.
- **No more pop-up messages.** The strikes' messages over the hotbar (DIRECT HIT, METEOR STORM INBOUND and the rest), STRIKE IN PROGRESS, STRIKE STOPPED and the wheel's "… LOADED" lines are gone, and so are the MESSAGES switch and `/orbital messages`. Two things still show over the hotbar: the designator's countdown while you hold it on a target (ACQUIRING TARGET, SEISMIC SCAN, DESIGNATING, and TRACKING STORM for the Hurricane), and NO TARGET IN RANGE. Firing while a strike is under way plays a dull click instead, and `/orbital stop` answers in chat. The wheel's right column moves up to fill the gap.
- **KNOCKBACK** now also switches the Hurricane's carry, and shows on the wheel for it.

## Fixes

- **macOS: the warning after a crash now shows** ([#3](https://github.com/Stink-O/orbital-laser/issues/3)). If the game closed while it was building a laser's effects, the next start loads the Plasma and says so in chat. That message was sent before the player was in the world, and on a Mac it was dropped; it now waits until you're in.
- The Supercell's rain close by is drawn in the world round you, the way the Hurricane's is, so nothing sticks to the picture as you turn.

## Install

1. **Get the Orbital Laser build of Horizon, `orbital-mod-api-10` or newer.** It's a new build: download `horizon-orbital-linux-x86_64.tar.gz` from [Stink-O/horizon releases](https://github.com/Stink-O/horizon/releases) and unpack it to `~/horizon-orbital`, replacing the old one. Prepare its resources once from your own Minecraft 26.3 (`./horizon-prepare --prepare --server-jar PATH/TO/server.jar`), or point `HORIZON_RESOURCES` at the prepared resources of a Horizon you already have. **The mod won't start on `orbital-mod-api-9` or older.**
2. Download **orbital-laser-v0.5.8.zip** below, unzip it, and with the game closed run `python3 install.py`.
3. Run `./play.sh` (a creative test world) or `./play.sh MyWorld`.
4. In the game, type `/orbital give`. Press **R** (or right-click the Orbital Remote) for the laser wheel, and hold right-click with the Designator on a target to fire. The Hurricane is on the wheel's third page.

**Want an AI agent to do it?** Paste the prompt from the [README](https://github.com/Stink-O/orbital-laser#set-it-up-with-an-ai-agent) into your coding agent.

> [!NOTE]
> **A native version is in the works.** Orbital Laser is being ported to Horizon's own native mods, with mixins for the hooks it still needs, so it will run on regular Horizon without the fork. The hooks are going into Horizon itself ([horizon-rust/horizon#5727](https://github.com/horizon-rust/horizon/issues/5727)). Until then, use the fork build as above.

> [!WARNING]
> **The Hurricane is permanent.** It strips everything standing round its eyewall, out to about 150 blocks for trees and buildings (everything built within about 100 goes), and leaves the trees, pieces of buildings and rubble it throws lying where they land. Only fire it in a throwaway world, or switch terrain damage off on the wheel (it still carries you).

> [!WARNING]
> **Early and experimental.** Developed and tested on one Linux PC (Fedora, NVIDIA). Reported working on Windows 11 by a user (the fork built from source, with `--features mods`). On macOS (Apple Silicon) the Downfall may run without its effects (see the README's Known gaps). The ready-built Horizon is Linux x86-64 only.
