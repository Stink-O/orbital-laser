## New

- **Nuke**, on the wheel's third page and `/orbital nuke`:
  - **the fall:** a re-entry streak across the sky, then the bomb's dive, faster and faster;
  - **the burst:** a white-out on the ground, a fireball boiling up into a mushroom cloud that folds over on itself, a condensation ring, the cloud's shadow on the land, and a shockwave you can see racing out in a wall of dust;
  - **the sound:** a slightly overdriven boom that arrives late with the distance, then rumble and falling debris;
  - **the feel:** the shockwave knocks you up and back, the camera shakes hard and soft, out-of-focus muck lands on the lens;
  - **the land:** a terraced crater about 200 blocks across and 40 deep with a glassy floor and a broken rim, the land flattened out to about 230 with the trees felled pointing away, and flash burns out to about 480 (what the land or a wall hid from the fireball left as it was). It goes in as one wave from the middle out, and works on far-off targets in the distant terrain, going in as the land there loads;
  - the strike lasts 90 seconds.
- **Payload:** its cloud casts a shadow on the land, muck on the lens is soft and out of focus, its land goes in from the middle out, and its craters cut into water naturally instead of behind sealed walls.
- **No more "took too long".** A work meter keeps every call well inside the game's limit, so no laser stops the mod. The classic lasers plan over a few ticks, the Planet Killer and the Downfall are split up, and firing a Crystal Bloom or a Planet Killer into a shaft to bedrock no longer crashes.
- **Smoother.** Block edits are paced by the frame time, and the fork's new build handles re-sent chunks far more cheaply, so big terrain waves stutter much less.

## Install

1. **Get the Orbital Laser build of Horizon, `orbital-mod-api-8`.** This release needs it: it adds `world.push-player` (the Nuke's knockback), and **the mod won't start on `orbital-mod-api-7` or older.** Download `horizon-orbital-linux-x86_64.tar.gz` from [Stink-O/horizon releases](https://github.com/Stink-O/horizon/releases) and unpack it to `~/horizon-orbital`, replacing the old one. Prepare its resources once from your own Minecraft 26.3 (`./horizon-prepare --prepare --server-jar PATH/TO/server.jar`), or point `HORIZON_RESOURCES` at the prepared resources of a Horizon you already have.
2. Download **orbital-laser-v0.5.6.zip** below, unzip it, and with the game closed run `python3 install.py`.
3. Run `./play.sh` (a creative test world) or `./play.sh MyWorld`.
4. In the game, type `/orbital give`. Press **R** (or right-click the Orbital Remote) for the laser wheel, and hold right-click with the Designator on a target to fire. The Nuke is on the wheel's third page.

**Want an AI agent to do it?** Paste the prompt from the [README](https://github.com/Stink-O/orbital-laser#set-it-up-with-an-ai-agent) into your coding agent.

> [!WARNING]
> **The Nuke is permanent, and huge.** It digs a crater about 200 blocks across and 40 deep, flattens everything standing out to about 230, strips and knocks down trees out to about 300, kills every mob within 300 and scorches what faces the flash out to about 480. **Stand well back** (500 blocks or more) and only fire it in a throwaway world, or switch terrain damage off on the wheel.

> [!WARNING]
> **Early and experimental.** Developed and tested on one Linux PC (Fedora, NVIDIA). Windows and macOS are untested, and the ready-built Horizon is Linux x86-64 only.
