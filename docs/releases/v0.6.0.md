## Install

1. **Get the Orbital Laser build of Horizon** (0.6.0 needs the new one, `orbital-mod-api-3`: it runs on Horizon's new renderer, and flying speed and typing on the mod's screen are new in its mod API): download `horizon-orbital-linux-x86_64.tar.gz` from [Stink-O/horizon releases](https://github.com/Stink-O/horizon/releases) and unpack it to `~/horizon-orbital`. Prepare its resources once from your own Minecraft 26.3 (`./horizon-prepare --prepare --server-jar PATH/TO/server.jar`), or point `HORIZON_RESOURCES` at the prepared resources of a Horizon you already have.
2. Download **orbital-laser-v0.6.0.zip** below, unzip it, and with the game closed run `python3 install.py`.
3. Run `./play.sh` (a creative test world) or `./play.sh MyWorld`.
4. In the game, type `/orbital give`. Press **R** (or right-click the Orbital Remote) for the laser wheel, and hold right-click with the Designator on a target to fire. The Downfall is on the wheel's second page.

**Stuck, or want an AI agent to do it?** Paste the prompt from the [README](https://github.com/Stink-O/orbital-laser#set-it-up-with-an-ai-agent) into your coding agent.

**Needs:** the Orbital Laser build of Horizon (with your own copy of Minecraft Java 26.3) and Python 3.8 or newer for the installer.

> [!WARNING]
> **A strike is destructive and permanent.** Every laser digs a crater and a shaft to bedrock and kills the mobs round it. **The Downfall is the biggest yet:** it buries a kilometre-long wreck at least 360 blocks in front of you, crushes the land under its whole length, heaps the ground it displaced up along both its sides, and its black hole leaves a crater with arms out to 150 blocks and a shaft to bedrock. The crushing carries on for up to a minute after the strike. Back up your world first, or switch terrain damage off on the wheel.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Stock Horizon can't run this mod.** Horizon's mod API doesn't yet let a mod catch an item's use, open its own screen, draw effects or change the world. The [Orbital Laser build](https://github.com/Stink-O/horizon/tree/mod-api-input-ui-render-world) is a Horizon fork that adds those four, from Horizon's own roadmap. On stock Horizon, `python3 install.py --legacy` sets up 0.1's way of doing it instead.

> [!WARNING]
> **Early and experimental.** Developed and tested on one Linux PC (Fedora, NVIDIA). Windows and macOS are untested, and the ready-built Horizon is Linux x86-64 only.

## New

- **Downfall**, the sixth laser, on the wheel's second page and `/orbital downfall`:
  - the Mothership drops in and charges, then the charge is cut and its power thrown to the turrets along its roof as a meteor comes in, first a point of light far off, then a burning, tumbling rock;
  - the turrets turn on their rings, lay their twin barrels on it and fire, and the rock breaks up past the ship's bow;
  - the pieces hit it anyway: fire bursts through the hull, chunks come away burning, and it lurches down as its lift gives, then falls free;
  - its stern strikes, its bow slams down and the hull shudders, blasts bursting along it and earth flung up in sheets, until it is buried half its height, crushing what it landed on;
  - then its black hole engine fails and ruptures, tearing the wreck apart piece by piece and drawing it in, before it collapses in a flash and bores a shaft to bedrock.
- **A second page on the wheel**, turned with its arrows, its tabs or a right-click.
- **A colour for each ship:** the Mothership green, the Downfall red, or amber, blue, violet or white, from the swatches beside the arrival row or `/orbital colour …`.
- **Faster flying** for getting round the craters: the FLY SPEED bar over the wheel's title (1x to 40x) and the box beside it (type any speed up to 1000x), or `/orbital flyspeed N`.
- **Switches for the clutter:** MESSAGES turns off the strikes' messages over the hotbar, and PANEL hides the status panel (`/orbital messages off`, `/orbital hud off`).
- **`/orbital skip SECONDS`** starts the next strike part way in, with terrain damage off, to look at one moment of it.
- **Every blast now clears to the sky:** a strike under a hill or a mountain no longer leaves a tunnel or floating hilltops.
- **No more "it took too long":** a strike's heavier world changes are worked out a little each tick.
- **On Horizon's new renderer.** Horizon replaced its shader-pack support, and the fork now gives a mod its own post pass on the new renderer; the effects look as they did.
- **A new logo.**
