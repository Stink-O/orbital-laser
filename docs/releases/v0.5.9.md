A tidy-up release: one item instead of two, R only with that item in hand, and strikes that end when you leave the world.

<!-- DRAFT. Shipped first as the native build: the native session fills in the native install steps and assets below. -->

## Changes

- **The Orbital Remote is removed.** The Designator is the mod's one item now. `/orbital give` gives you the Designator, and the laser wheel opens with **R** while you hold it, or with `/orbital wheel` from anywhere. A Remote already in a world's inventory loads as an empty slot: it does nothing and breaks nothing.
- **R opens the wheel only with the Designator in hand.** With anything else in hand the mod ignores R, so the key is free for whatever else you use it for.
- **Leaving the world mid-strike ends the strike.** Before, a strike carried on into the next world you opened. Now its plan, the land changes still waiting and the sounds still to come are dropped as you leave. The chunks it forceloaded are let go the next time you play that world (about two seconds in), also if the game was closed mid-strike, so the save isn't left holding them.
- **Pausing holds the strike.** The pause menu stops the strike's clock, its land changes and its effects, and the game pauses its sounds and picks them up where they were when you go back.

## Known gap

- **Sounds can play on over the title screen.** Horizon doesn't stop the sounds that are playing when you Save and Quit to Title, its own included (a music disc keeps going too). So a strike's sounds can carry on there until they end. The mod can't stop them once the world is gone; it needs a fix in Horizon.

## Install

1. **Get the Orbital Laser build of Horizon, `orbital-mod-api-10` or newer** (the same as 0.5.8), from [Stink-O/horizon releases](https://github.com/Stink-O/horizon/releases).
2. Download **orbital-laser-v0.5.9.zip** below, unzip it, and with the game closed run `python3 install.py`.
3. Run `./play.sh` (a creative test world) or `./play.sh MyWorld`.
4. In the game, type `/orbital give`. Hold the Designator and press **R** for the laser wheel, and hold right-click with it on a target to fire.

**Want an AI agent to do it?** Paste the prompt from the [README](https://github.com/Stink-O/orbital-laser#set-it-up-with-an-ai-agent) into your coding agent.

> [!CAUTION]
> **Photosensitivity:** the Hurricane's eyewall lightning, the Rod from God's impact frames (on by default; `/orbital impactframes off`), the Supercell's finale and other strikes' lightning and blasts are bright flashes.

> [!WARNING]
> **Strikes with terrain damage on are permanent.** Only fire them in a throwaway world, or switch terrain damage off on the wheel.
