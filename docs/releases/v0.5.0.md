## Install

1. **Get the Orbital Laser build of Horizon** (0.5.0 needs the new one, `orbital-mod-api-2`: the camera shake and the longer raycast are new in its mod API): download `horizon-orbital-linux-x86_64.tar.gz` from [Stink-O/horizon releases](https://github.com/Stink-O/horizon/releases) and unpack it to `~/horizon-orbital`. Prepare its resources once from your own Minecraft 26.3 (`./horizon-prepare --prepare --server-jar PATH/TO/server.jar`), or point `HORIZON_RESOURCES` at the prepared resources of a Horizon you already have.
2. Download **orbital-laser-v0.5.0.zip** below, unzip it, and with the game closed run `python3 install.py`.
3. Run `./play.sh` (a creative test world) or `./play.sh MyWorld`.
4. In the game, type `/orbital give`. Press **R** (or right-click the Orbital Remote) for the laser wheel, and hold right-click with the Designator on a target to fire.

**Stuck, or want an AI agent to do it?** Paste the prompt from the [README](https://github.com/Stink-O/orbital-laser#set-it-up-with-an-ai-agent) into your coding agent.

**Needs:** the Orbital Laser build of Horizon (with your own copy of Minecraft Java 26.3) and Python 3.8 or newer for the installer.

> [!WARNING]
> **A strike is destructive and permanent.** It flattens everything above the ground within about 44 blocks (52 for the Black Hole), digs a crater about 60 blocks across and a shaft down to bedrock, and kills every mob and dropped item within 40 blocks (players are spared). **The Meteor Storm is far bigger:** craters across 120 blocks, everything above the ground flattened out to 100 blocks, a crater about 125 blocks across, the land charred out to 135, rubble thrown out to 250 and every mob within 130 blocks killed. **The Mothership** vaporises the land out to 34 blocks, digs a crater 22 across and a shaft to bedrock, and cuts a trench 100 blocks long, up to 15 wide and 17 deep, away from you. Back up your world first, or switch terrain damage off on the wheel.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Stock Horizon can't run this mod.** Horizon's mod API doesn't yet let a mod catch an item's use, open its own screen, draw effects or change the world. The [Orbital Laser build](https://github.com/Stink-O/horizon/tree/mod-api-input-ui-render-world) is a Horizon fork that adds those four, from Horizon's own roadmap. On stock Horizon, `python3 install.py --legacy` sets up 0.1's way of doing it instead.

> [!WARNING]
> **Early and experimental.** Developed and tested on one Linux PC (Fedora, NVIDIA). Windows and macOS are untested, and the ready-built Horizon is Linux x86-64 only.

## New

- **Mothership**, the fifth laser, on the wheel and `/orbital mothership`:
  - a slab of dark metal over a kilometre long drops out of hyperspace and stops dead over the target; the air ripples, the land dims under its shadow and its green lights cascade on from stern to bow;
  - its emitters charge and converge under its eye, and a green beam with a white-hot core drills a shaft to bedrock;
  - the beam sweeps away from you, cutting a molten trench 100 blocks long that crusts over and steams as it cools;
  - then it pulls away into a streak, and its boom follows it out.
- **Pick the approach and the arrival** on the wheel, in two rows under the ring: ahead, behind, left, right or straight down from above; and a film-style snap, unfolding out of a point, or out of a warp streak. Also `/orbital approach …` and `/orbital arrival …`.
- **Range out to 1000 blocks** for the Mothership. Past your render distance it aims across Horizon's distant terrain, so turn on **LOD Distance** in Video Settings for the full range.
- **Real camera shake on every laser.** The old screen jitter is gone: the game turns the camera itself, so the world, your hand and the effects move together, and each jolt lands with the sound, weaker the further off you are.
- **The ground breaking is heard from the impact**, not in your ears wherever you are.
- The Mothership is mod-only: the 0.1 setup for stock Horizon still has Plasma and Godly.
