# Recordings used by tools/sounds_earthquake.py (the Earthquake's breaking rock)

Both are CC0 (public domain dedication) on Freesound, used here as the MP3 previews Freesound serves. The `syn_*` take's
rock breaks in the air and the blows' stone (every `fracture` of rock that is not a dark roll through the ground) are
breaks cut from them, played as recorded (a resample of at most a third for the size, a low-pass only where the break
is far or heard through the ground), with distortion on their mids (`CRUNCH_*`) and a synthesized thud under the big
ones. Everything else in the take is synthesized.

| File | Title | Author | Source | Licence | What is used |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| rock_destroy_524312.mp3 | Rock destroy | Bertsz | https://freesound.org/s/524312/ | CC0 | every break (found by its onset), 4 ms before it to 0.28 s on or the next break |
| rockcrack03_489905.mp3 | RockCrack03 | falcospizaetus | https://freesound.org/s/489905/ | CC0 | the same, its louder breaks |

The cuts are `grains` in tools/sounds_earthquake.py.
