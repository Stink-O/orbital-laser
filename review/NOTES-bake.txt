THE SKY BREAKS' HAND, A TRIAL: a sculpted hand and arm baked into per-bone distance grids
branch sb-bake (base 4d811af), worktree ~/.cache/orbital-sb-bake. Not pushed, not merged, not installed, never run in the game.

Sheets: review/bake-compare.jpg (clay, his own hand / sculpted / rounded shapes, six poses and views)
        review/bake-strike-views.jpg (the strike's own views hdpclose, hdslam, hdslamclose at 9.3, 44.5, 46.2 s, both hands)


1. WHAT IT IS

MakeHuman's base mesh (CC0), its right arm, subdivided twice (Catmull-Clark), fitted to the skeleton hd_set already poses
(its knuckles, its fingers' lengths, the thumb's root), cut at the joints into 18 rigid pieces. Each piece is a small grid
of distances in its own bone's frame, ten bits a sample, three samples a word. The pass reads a piece's grid where its
bone is now, so the one pose (hd_set, unchanged) drives the sculpted hand and the hand of rounded shapes alike.
HD_BAKE (skybreak.wgsl) picks the sculpted one; with the grids missing from mod_data the pass draws the rounded one.


2. THE PIPELINE, AND HOW TO RUN IT AGAIN

    python3 -I tools/bake_hand.py ~/.cache/orbital-handmesh/src [--look DIR]        (about a minute, numpy only)

writes  mod/assets/sb_hand.bin     the grids (389,212 bytes; include_bytes! in mod/src/strike/sb_hand.rs)
        mod/shaders/skybreak.wgsl  the block between `// [bake table]` and `// [end bake table]`: hb_piece (each grid's
                                   offset, samples, first sample, spacing), HB_WORDS, HB_RAD, HB_PLO/HB_PHI, HB_RF/HB_RU,
                                   HB_UZ, HB_FZ. The two go together: bake, then rebuild.
Nothing runs at build time. `-I` because the mesh folder is data, not code. Then:

    tools/check-shader.sh
    cd mod && cargo test --release sb_
    tools/preview.py skybreak 9.3 44.5 --views hdpclose,hdslam --set hdclay=1            (the sculpted one)
    ... --set hdbake=0     the hand of rounded shapes          ... --set hdtint=1     each piece in its own colour
    ... --set hdlab=1 hdpose=I,M,R,L,THUMB,SPREAD,TURN,TIP     a fixed pose in the lab

Steps inside the bake (tools/bake_hand.py): load, arm_faces (the body's faces weighted over half to the arm's bones),
catmull_clark twice, Fit (a similarity fit of the mesh's joints to the shader's, then a warp so every joint lands on the
shader's own), Sdf (exact distance to the triangles, signed), Pieces (the cuts, below), build, pack, main.

4. THE MOD'S DATA: LAYOUT AND OFFSETS            (for the merge with sb-anim)

    word 24,576  SB_HD   (shader HD)        the hand's 16 words (SB_HDW)                              unchanged
    word 24,592  SB_HP   (shader HD_HP)     the pose table: on this branch 22 words a pose (SB_HP_W),
                                            136 poses from 8.4 s then 277 from 40.8 s = 9,086 words,
                                            ending at word 33,678                                     unchanged
    word 65,536  SB_HB   (shader HB)        the grids: [0x53424844, 1, 18 pieces, 97,303 words in all], then the grids;
                                            ends at word 162,839                                      NEW

THE GRIDS' OFFSETS DO NOT DEPEND ON THE POSE TABLE'S LENGTH. SB_HB is a fixed word, and every grid's offset in hb_piece
is counted from HB, not from the table's end. The one condition: the pose table must end below word 65,536.
sb-anim's table (199 + 277 = 476 poses of 26 words = 12,376 words) ends at word 36,968: fine as it is, nothing of mine
to change. There is room for 1,574 poses of 26 words before the two would meet; nothing checks that today (a const
assert `SB_HP + poses * SB_HP_W <= SB_HB` in sb_hand.rs would).
Where it is set: sb_hand.rs `SB_HB`, `SB_HAND_BIN`, `sb_hand_grids()`, and in `sb_hand_plan` one
`Act::Words { index: SB_HB, .. }` at 0.07 s; skybreak.rs `sb_write_table` (the preview's data) resizes to SB_HB and
appends the grids; skybreak.wgsl `HB`, `HB_WORDS`.
The pass draws the sculpted hand only if arrayLength(&mod_data) >= HB + HB_WORDS and the heading's mark and length match
(hd_set, hd_trace); otherwise the hand of rounded shapes, as before.
The fork takes at most 262,144 words in one render.set-data (POST_DATA_WORDS): 162,839 fits. The mod sends the whole
array each time a word of it changes (lib.rs), so each such send is now 651 KB where it was about 135 KB.

KEEP hd_set FILLING hd_fb AND hd_kn. It does, for both hands, as before: hd_kn in the loop over the fingers (about line
2529), hd_fb for all 15 bones. The sculpted hand reads hd_fb itself (hb_bone); sb-anim's hd_shade can go on reading both.

5. THE JOINTS, AND WHAT WAS DONE AT EACH

The suggested way was to cut by bone weight and smooth-union the pieces. Cut by weight the pieces' ends were ragged and
the bent joints opened into gaps or stood out as balls, so:
  - Cuts are by geometry: planes through the webs between the fingers (a column a finger), a sleeve and a ball round the
    thumb, the wrist, the elbow. Pieces overlap, so the rest pose is the sculpt itself, with no seam to hide.
  - A piece's end is its own section there swept round the joint's axis (`hinged`), so a bent joint stays as thick as the
    finger is. Knuckles: a low mound on the back of each (PALM_MOUND, FINGER_MOUND) and a saddle between two fingers
    (SADDLE), not balls.
  - A finger's root is clipped round along it and to a ball behind its knuckle: the mesh's finger carries the palm's pad
    a third of the way up, and bent that stood out of the fist as a block.
  - Fingers: a finger's own three pieces smooth-union with each other, its root with the palm; never a finger with its
    neighbour or the thumb. Shut, a finger's end smooth-unions with its own root and the palm (the coil, filled).
  - Thumb: the palm and the thumb's first piece both hold the ball of the thumb, and the palm keeps the whole back of the
    hand and the web down to WEB_FLOOR (a shell there was a fin once the thumb swung into the fist). Union radius
    0.4 + 1.8 * thumb's curl.
  - Palm: closing, its back arches with the knuckles hd_set sinks (hd_cv), on the ring's and the little finger's side.
  - Wrist: not a union but a cross-fade between the forearm's grid and the palm's, over z -33 to -21 (both hold that
    stretch of the sculpt), so the wrist is one skin.
  - Elbow: forearm and upper arm smooth-union (1.2 units). Past their grids' ends both run on as they are there.
  - Outside a grid, a bound that is never more than the truth (hb_grid's `mrg`); under the hand's box, down the arm, the
    hand is worked out exactly within 8 units (the light and the shadow ask for distances well off the skin; a bound
    there drew a dark line round the wrist).
  - Normals: the taps widened to the grids' spacing (hd_ne), or the skin shows the samples.


6. COST

NOT MEASURED ON THE GPU. The game was running for all of the close-out but one short window (one frame drawn), and the
rule is no GPU work while it is. To measure, with the game closed, one job at a time under flock ~/.cache/orbital-gpu.lock:
    tools/preview.py skybreak 9.3 10.6 42.6 43.2 44.5 46.2 --views sbplayer,hdpclose,hdslam,hdslamfar,hdslamclose \
        --size 1920x1080 --bench                        and the same with --set hdbake=0
(the rounded hand's figures on sb-hand3, same method: worst about 2.8 ms at 46.2 s hdslam, 2.57 ms at 9.3 s hdpclose).

What there is: the whole pass on the CPU renderer (lavapipe), 480x270, clay, median of 9, ms. A ratio at best: a GPU
pays differently for the grids' reads than a CPU does.
                              sculpted   rounded
     9.3 s  hdpclose            35.8       38.6
     9.3 s  hdslam              30.2       30.2
    44.5 s  hdpclose            18.4       18.7
    44.5 s  hdslam              24.9       25.1
    44.5 s  hdslamclose         41.7       45.2
    46.2 s  hdpclose            21.1       22.3
    46.2 s  hdslam              59.1       54.3
    46.2 s  hdslamclose         65.1       64.9      (no hand in view: the noise is about this much)
So on the CPU the sculpted hand costs the pass about what the rounded one does: 9% more at worst (46.2 s hdslam), 8%
less at best. Whether a GPU agrees is the open question.

By count: a grid sample is 8 words read (four rows, two words each) and three mixes. One hd_map near the hand reads the
palm, the forearm, the upper arm, the thumb's three and each finger whose ball the point is near: 6 grids at least, 18 at
most, 48 to 144 words read. Away from the hand's ball only the two arm grids (16 words); the ray is clipped to the hand's
ball and the arm's two capsules first.
The mod: mod.wasm 6,577 KB (the grids are 389 KB of it). sb_hand_grids runs once a strike at 0.07 s and counts 12 a word
with the meter (1.17 M of a tick's 50 M). mod_data grows to 162,839 words (651 KB), sent whole on each change.

8. WHAT IS STILL WRONG                             (bluntly; the sheets show all of it)

  - The fist from the side: a dark cavity in the coil of the fingers and a muddled thumb across it. The rounded hand is
    cleaner from there. It shows in the strike too: 9.3 s hdpclose has a dimple by the thumb on the punching fist.
  - (Fixed, see 11.) The elbow, close (44.5 and 46.2 s hdpclose): a ridge where the forearm's and the upper arm's pieces
    met, and a bulge on the upper arm just over it.
  - The arm reads thinner than the rounded one's (44.5 s hdslam): a slim wrist and forearm under a big hand. It is the
    sculpt's own proportion; whether that suits a giant's arm is a matter of taste.
  - The knuckle row, close: low mounds and saddles instead of balls, which is the point, but still lumpier than his own
    fist, with faint lines where the pieces' caps meet and the little finger's knuckle a doubled lump.
  - The open hand's fingers show their joints as rings (the caps' mounds), more than a real hand does.
  - Not looked at: a bent wrist held still, the palm's faceting at 0.9 spacing in a grazing light, the skin (creases,
    nails, colour: only clay was judged), the far view from the player, the hand in the game.
  - No bake of the left hand: it is the right hand mirrored (hd_h.side), as the rounded one is.
  - review/work/motion.py in this worktree is not mine and is not committed.

Fixed in the close-out: the square line on the back of the hand (the palm's grid stopped short of the wrist and the
hand's edge: d_palm's floor in the bake), and the dark line round the wrist (hd_map_b's bound under the hand's box).
